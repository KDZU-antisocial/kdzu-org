Static is the Spellwork is a participatory workshop that shares KDZU’s practice of tactical mythmaking. DJ Future Ghost (Rob Ray) will guide participants through the MIX Center and its surrounding terrain revealing how our technology-infused environment can become sites of collectivity, activated histories, and autofictions waiting to emerge.
